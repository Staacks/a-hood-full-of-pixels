# Sound sources

`tools/make_sounds.py` turns these files into the scene sounds on the controller's SD card
(`media/sd_controller/*.wav`). The other scene sounds are synthesised by the script.

The following clips are from [freesound.org](https://freesound.org) and were released by their
authors under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), i.e.
into the public domain. No attribution is required, but thanks to the authors anyway:

| File | Title | Author | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `392697__poligonstudio__bass-growl.wav` | [Bass Growl](https://freesound.org/people/poligonstudio/sounds/392697/) | Poligonstudio | eyes |
| `524305__bertsz__wet-spell-shoot.wav` | [Wet Spell shoot](https://freesound.org/people/bertsz/sounds/524305/) | Bertsz | fire skull |
| `812748__audiopapkin__sound-design-elements-impact-sfx-ps-082.wav` | [Sound Design Elements Impact SFX PS 082](https://freesound.org/people/audiopapkin/sounds/812748/) | AudioPapkin | matrix |
| `812709__audiopapkin__sound-design-elements-impact-sfx-ps-043.wav` | [Sound Design Elements Impact SFX PS 043](https://freesound.org/people/audiopapkin/sounds/812709/) | AudioPapkin | lightning skull |

`signature.wav` is the jingle of my YouTube channel and is used for the "me" scene. It is
covered by the licence for media files in this repository (see the README).
